# Known limitations

- Caesar Cipher is not suitable for real confidentiality.
- Brute-force candidates are not automatically ranked by linguistic correctness.
- Letter-frequency assumptions are language- and sample-dependent.
- SQLite history is local and does not provide tamper-evident audit guarantees.
- The app is a learning tool and has not undergone a formal penetration test.
- A passing unit-test suite cannot prove that the whole application is secure.
